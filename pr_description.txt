### 💡 What
Added `.accessibilityAddTraits(.isButton)` to the custom interactive list items in `ClipCardView`.

### 🎯 Why
In SwiftUI, when `.accessibilityElement(children: .ignore)` is applied to a view to group its contents and hide individual children from VoiceOver, it also strips the view of any implicit interactive traits (even if the view functions as a button or has an `onTapGesture`). This means VoiceOver users could navigate to the card but wouldn't hear that it's actionable.

### 📸 Before/After
- **Before:** VoiceOver reads the clip contents but does not announce the element as a button.
- **After:** VoiceOver announces the clip contents followed by "Button", and properly handles the `accessibilityHint` ("Click to copy to clipboard").

### ♿ Accessibility
This restores basic interactive semantics (the "isButton" trait) to the primary UI component, ensuring blind and low-vision users using VoiceOver know the list items can be activated.
