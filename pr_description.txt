💡 **What:** Added `.accessibilityAddTraits(.isButton)` to `ClipCardView` to explicitly mark it as an interactive element.
🎯 **Why:** The existing `.accessibilityElement(children: .ignore)` modifier is necessary to provide a clean, custom accessibility label instead of reading every child element separately. However, this modifier strips all implicit interaction traits from the view. Even though the card is wrapped in a `Button` inside `ContentView`, VoiceOver fails to announce it as an actionable button, leaving screen reader users unaware that they can click the row to copy the item.
📸 **Before/After:** No visual changes.
♿ **Accessibility:** VoiceOver now correctly announces the card as a "Button", restoring the missing semantic meaning and interaction cue.
