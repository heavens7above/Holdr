💡 **What**: Added the `.isButton` accessibility trait to custom list items (`ClipCardView`).
🎯 **Why**: When overriding the accessibility tree in SwiftUI with `.accessibilityElement(children: .ignore)` (necessary to prevent VoiceOver from reading every sub-element of the card individually), the implicit button interaction traits are lost, even when wrapped in a `Button` in the parent view. Adding `.accessibilityAddTraits(.isButton)` restores the semantic meaning so screen readers announce the element as an actionable button rather than static text.
📸 **Before/After**: No visual change; improves audio VoiceOver experience.
♿ **Accessibility**: VoiceOver now correctly announces clip history items as actionable, enhancing usability for visually impaired users.
